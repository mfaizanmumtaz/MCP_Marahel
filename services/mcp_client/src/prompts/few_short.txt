# Few-Shot Examples for Breastfeeding Knowledge Base

## Arabic Examples (الأمثلة العربية)

### Example 1: General Breastfeeding Information
**User Query (Arabic):** ما هي فوائد الرضاعة الطبيعية للطفل؟
**Response:** هناك العديد من فوائد الرضاعة الطبيعية للطفل منها:
- يحتوي حليب الأم على كل المغذيات التي يحتاجها الرضيع
- سهولة هضم حليب الأم مما يمكن الرضيع من الاستفادة منه بصورة مثالية
- يحمي الطفل الرضيع من العدوى لاحتوائه على أجسام مضادة
- يساعد حليب الأم على النمو العقلي والذهني للطفل
- حليب الأم جاهز للرضاعة دائما ولا يحتاج إلى تحضير أو تدفئة

### Example 2: Proper Latching Technique
**User Query (Arabic):** كيف يكون التقام فم الطفل الرضيع للثدي بشكل صحيح؟
**Response:** تحرص الأم المرضع عند التقام فم الطفل الرضيع للثدي أن يكون:
- فتح الطفل لفمه جيداً
- ملامسة ذقن الطفل للثدي
- انقلاب شفته السفلى نحو الخارج
- زيادة مساحة الهالة فوق فم الطفل عنها في أسفله

### Example 3: Milk Production Hormones
**User Query (Arabic):** ما هي الهرمونات المسؤولة عن إنتاج وتدفق حليب الأم؟
**Response:**
- **هرمون البرولاكتين:** يسمى بهرمون الحليب وهو المسؤول عن إفراز الحليب
- **هرمون الأوكسيتوسين:** يسمى بهرمون تدفق الحليب وهو المسؤول عن تدفق الحليب قبل الإرضاع وأثناء الرضاعة

### Example 4: Increasing Milk Supply
**User Query (Arabic):** ماذا أفعل لزيادة كمية إدرار الحليب لدي؟
**Response:** هناك طرق طبيعية وسهلة لزيادة الحليب:
- البدء في الرضاعة الطبيعية بعد الولادة مباشرة
- الإرضاع قدر المستطاع طوال اليوم مع التركيز على الرضاعة الليلية
- إفراغ الثدي بشكل مستمر من خلال الاعتصار اليدوي أو الشفط
- شرب ما لا يقل عن 3 لتر ماء يومياً
- تناول المشروبات الساخنة مثل الحلبة واليانسون والكراوية

### Example 5: Breast Problems During Breastfeeding
**User Query (Arabic):** ما هي المشاكل التي يتعرض لها الثدي أثناء الرضاعة الطبيعية؟
**Response:** المشاكل الشائعة تشمل:
1. **الاحتقان (تحجر الثدي):** ثديان أكبر حجماً وأكثر ثقلاً وحساسية
   - *العلاج:* الإرضاع فوراً، كمادات دافئة، مساج للثدي، كمادات باردة بعد الرضاعة
2. **ألم الحلمة:** ألم أثناء الرضاعة مع تشققات محتملة
   - *العلاج:* تصحيح الالتقام، دهن الحلمة بحليب الأم، تجنب الصابون
3. **القناة المسدودة:** نتوء حساس ومؤلم
   - *العلاج:* كمادات دافئة، تدليك الثدي، إرضاع من الثدي المتأثر أولاً

## English Examples (الأمثلة الإنجليزية)

### Example 1: General Breastfeeding Benefits
**User Query (English):** What are the benefits of breastfeeding for the baby?
**Response:** Breastfeeding provides numerous benefits for the baby including:
- Breast milk contains all the nutrients the infant needs
- Easy digestion allows the baby to benefit optimally from breast milk
- Protects the baby from infections due to antibodies
- Supports mental and cognitive development
- Breast milk is always ready and requires no preparation or warming
- Prevents allergies that may be caused by artificial feeding

### Example 2: Proper Breastfeeding Position
**User Query (English):** How should I position my baby for proper breastfeeding?
**Response:** For correct breastfeeding position, ensure:
- The baby's mouth opens wide
- The baby's chin touches the breast
- The lower lip is turned outward
- More areola is visible above the baby's mouth than below
- Both mother and baby maintain straight backs
- The baby faces and is close to the mother's chest

### Example 3: Signs of Adequate Milk Intake
**User Query (English):** How do I know if my baby is getting enough milk?
**Response:** Two important indicators show your baby is getting enough milk:
- The baby wets diapers 6-8 times per day (breast milk is 87% water)
- Noticeable weight gain and growth

Additional signs include:
- 8-12 feeding sessions per day
- 10-20 minutes per breast
- You can hear the baby swallowing
- Breasts feel empty and soft after effective feeding

### Example 4: Breast Problems During Breastfeeding
**User Query (English):** What breast problems might occur during breastfeeding?
**Response:** Common breast problems include:
1. **Engorgement:** Breasts become larger, heavier, sensitive, hard, and painful
   - *Treatment:* Immediate feeding, warm compresses, breast massage, cold compresses after feeding
2. **Nipple Pain:** Pain during feeding, possibly with cracks and bleeding
   - *Treatment:* Correct latching, apply remaining breast milk to nipples, avoid soap, use safe moisturizer
3. **Blocked Ducts:** Sensitive, painful lumps
   - *Treatment:* Warm compresses, breast massage, feed from affected breast first
4. **Breast Infection:** Fungal or bacterial infection
   - *Treatment:* Contact doctor immediately, continue emptying breasts, rest and increase fluid intake

### Example 5: Weaning Process
**User Query (English):** When and how should I wean my baby?
**Response:** According to WHO recommendations:
- **When:** Gradual introduction of solid foods at 6 months while continuing breastfeeding until 24 months
- **How:** Gradual weaning process:
  - Reduce feeding sessions every 2-5 days
  - Decrease duration of each session
  - Increase time between sessions
  - Many mothers stop daytime feeding first, keeping nighttime feeds last
- **Important:** Avoid stopping suddenly to prevent breast problems like engorgement or infection

### Example 6: Skin-to-Skin Contact
**User Query (English):** What is skin-to-skin contact and why is it important?
**Response:** Skin-to-skin contact involves placing the newborn without clothes on the mother's bare chest immediately after birth, covered together in a room with moderate temperature.

**Benefits include:**
- Enhanced bonding between mother and baby
- Earlier initiation and longer duration of breastfeeding
- Stimulation of milk production hormones (prolactin and oxytocin)
- Transfer of beneficial bacteria from mother to baby
- Temperature regulation for the baby
- Improved heart rate in the newborn

### Example 7: Milk Storage Guidelines
**User Query (English):** How should I store pumped breast milk?
**Response:** Proper breast milk storage guidelines:
- Use clean, sterilized, dry containers
- Store in small quantities (30-60ml) to avoid waste
- **Room temperature (25°C):** 4 hours maximum
- **Refrigerator (0-4°C):** Up to 8 days
- **Freezer (separate from refrigerator):** Up to 3 months
- **Cooler with ice packs:** 24 hours

**Thawing frozen milk:**
- Move from freezer to refrigerator 24 hours before use
- Use warm (not hot) water bath
- Gently swirl to mix (don't shake)
- Never refreeze or reheat thawed milk

### Example 8: Nipple Confusion
**User Query (English):** Why does my baby refuse breastfeeding and prefer the bottle?
**Response:** This is called "nipple confusion" or "nipple preference," which occurs when:
- The baby cannot latch properly to the breast after using artificial nipples
- The fast milk flow and hard texture of silicone nipples make breastfeeding difficult
- The baby prefers the easier flow from bottles

**Solutions:**
- Replace silicone nipples with alternatives (cup, spoon, syringe)
- Continue attempting direct breastfeeding with patience
- Practice skin-to-skin contact to encourage acceptance
- Maintain eye contact during feeding to build connection

### Example 9: Benefits for Mothers
**User Query (English):** What are the benefits of breastfeeding for mothers?
**Response:** Breastfeeding benefits for mothers include:
- Helps stop postpartum bleeding and returns uterus to pre-pregnancy size
- Reduces risk of ovarian and breast cancer
- Exclusive breastfeeding on demand can delay pregnancy during first 6 months
- Provides convenience - no preparation time needed
- Strengthens emotional bond with baby
- Cost-effective compared to formula feeding
- Burns calories, helping with postpartum weight loss

### Example 10: Introduction of Solid Foods
**User Query (English):** When should I introduce solid foods to my baby?
**Response:** WHO recommendations for introducing solid foods:
- **When:** Start at 6 months while continuing breastfeeding until 24 months
- **How:**
  - Introduce one food type at a time for several days to monitor allergic reactions
  - Start with cooked, well-mashed foods, gradually increasing texture
  - Begin with small amounts and gradually increase
  - Avoid forcing the baby to eat large quantities
  - Avoid salt, sugar, and spices initially
  - Gradually increase number and variety of meals throughout the day
